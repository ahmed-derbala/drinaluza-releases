## 📥 Download & install

1. Open the [latest release](https://github.com/ahmed-derbala/drinaluza-releases/releases/latest).
2. Download the `drinaluza-*.apk` file from the **Assets** section.
3. On your Android device, allow installs from your browser or file manager when prompted.
4. Open the downloaded APK and install it.

The app can also check for and download new versions from its **Updates** screen.

> **APK only for now.** Prefer the web version? Head to [drinaluza.com](https://drinaluza.com/).

## ✨ Features

**Marketplace**

- Create an account in seconds.
- Buy and sell seafood products and maritime equipment.
- Browse listings with photos, prices in Tunisian dinar (TND), and direct seller contact.

**Sea & coast**

- Rent jet skis, boat trips, and coastal houses.
- Weather tailored for swimming and fishing.
- Manage fishing crews and track boats.
- Talk with boats and crews with or without an internet connection.

**For businesses**

- Manage your products from a business dashboard.
- Share your business page so customers can call, message, or find you.
- Use the app in English, Tunisian Latin or Tunisian Arabic.
- Keep lists and photos available offline.
